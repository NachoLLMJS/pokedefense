Terrain is rendered by byte-identical copies of PokeSurvivor's core/util.js, world/tiles.js and world/world.js, including its original soil, water, trees, rocks and tall grass. Map seed 723, original forest biome 0, viewport origin (-480,-320). No tiles or props are redrawn, deleted or replaced. The dotted line is only a tactical route indicator over the original map. The source project generates maps procedurally, so there is no single fixed map image to copy. Same-seed terrain and prop parity is tested against PokeSurvivor itself.
Original synthesized sound module also copied unchanged from PokeSurvivor. The original sprite animation loader, types/pokedex/moves/starters catalogs, VFX/FX/projectiles and combat system are likewise copied byte-identically. skill-bridge.js adapts those original moves to stationary defenders and TD upgrades. The source Combat.resolve handles attack damage; the legacy engine attack path is disabled when this bridge is installed.
Game interface and tower-defense economy/wave orchestration are new. No credentials or backend dependencies.

Enemy assets: 60 normal species in five staged groups, plus three bosses (63 unique enemy species). Walk sheets are copied byte-identically from PokeSurvivor and browser-decoded in QA. Six ranged defenders use the original canonical starter abilities, including returning Razor Leaf and radial Bubble. No original source module was edited.
